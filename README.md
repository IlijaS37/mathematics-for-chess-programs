# Mathematics for chess programs
A research paper exploring mathematical concepts to the chess programs.

The paper was written during the 2022/2023 school year while I was a forth-year student at the "Uroš Predić" Grammar school in Pančevo and a student at the Regional Center for Talented Youth "Mihajlo Pupin" Pančevo.

The paper is written in Serbian and includes an abstract in English.
